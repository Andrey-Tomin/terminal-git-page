Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

All subscriptions are billed and managed safely directly by Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time via your Mac App Store account settings or via your Mac's system settings under your Apple ID Subscriptions panel.

Can I upgrade from monthly to Lifetime?

Yes. If you have an active monthly subscription, you can upgrade to the Lifetime License at any time from the app interface by clicking the active trial indicator or subscription settings button.

Are my SSH keys secure?

Absolutely. Your keys are encrypted locally using hardware-bound AES-256 and never leave your Mac. We do not have access to your keys, passwords, or servers.
